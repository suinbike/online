MÄMBET: til mäselesi

Director: Suinbike Suleimenova

Format: Docu-mockumentary

Status: In production

Logline:
An artist filmmaker discovers her native language in a country where language matter is a radical reality. In Kazakhstan, Kazakh-speaking and Russian-speaking communities co-exist separately across language bubbles. Qazaq new wave weaved with a POV from inside brings up pride, reclamation and controversy.
